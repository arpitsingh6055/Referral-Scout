We need **6 steps** to deliver a strong Empathy Exercise. No unnecessary documentation, no premature solution design.

## **Execution plan — DTI Empathy Exercise**

**1. Select participants**

Recruit **6–8 job seekers** who have used LinkedIn for referrals and **2–3 employees** who have received referral requests.

Include both active and discouraged job seekers, not just people who are successful at networking.

Output: Participant list with basic profiles.

**2. Prepare the fieldwork guide**

Create one short interview and observation guide aligned with the research questions.

Ask participants about **specific past experiences**, not hypothetical problems. Include a task where they demonstrate how they find a job, identify employees and retrieve an earlier referral conversation.

Output: Interview questions + Look–Ask–Try observation sheet.

**3. Conduct fieldwork and capture evidence**

Run **15–20-minute sessions** with job seekers and shorter interviews with employees.

Observe actual LinkedIn behaviour, ask about their experiences and try the referral process yourselves.

With consent, collect screen recordings, photographs, audio clips and field notes. Hide personal messages and identifying information where appropriate.

Output: Look–Ask–Try findings supported by real evidence.

**4. Analyse observations using AEIOU**

Organise what you observed into **Activities, Environments, Interactions, Objects and Users**.

Identify recurring friction, workarounds, differences between participants and unexpected findings.

Separate what participants *said* from what you actually *observed*.

Output: One AEIOU matrix + 3–5 evidence-backed insights.

**5. Build the journey map and refine the problem**

Map the candidate's experience from finding an opening to following up or stopping.

Show their actions, difficulties, emotions and workarounds at each relevant stage.

Use the findings to **refine the original problem statement**, even if the evidence contradicts our expectations.

Output: One journey map + final evidence-grounded problem statement.

**6. Prepare the 25-minute presentation**

Present the problem, research approach, real fieldwork evidence, AEIOU analysis, journey map and key insights.

End with **what you learned about the users**, not an application or feature proposal.

Output: Final presentation + supporting evidence folder.
