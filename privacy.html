Referral Scout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Referral Scout works inside your own browser. Your LinkedIn profile, CV, job lists and messages stay on your device. The only thing sent to us is one small usage record, and only after you agree to it. We do not sell data, show ads or track your browsing.

What Referral Scout is

Referral Scout is a browser extension that helps you find jobs on LinkedIn, rank them against your background, find people who could refer you, and draft connection notes and messages for you to approve.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only in the extension's own storage inside your browser. It is not sent to us.

  • Your LinkedIn profile text, which the extension reads when you ask it to.
  • The text of your CV, if you upload one.
  • Your target roles, location, qualifications and settings.
  • Jobs and job descriptions the extension reads, and how they were ranked.
  • People it finds on LinkedIn (their public name, headline and company), and the notes and messages drafted for them.
  • A history of what the extension did for you, such as invitations sent and replies received.

What we collect

Only after you accept the consent screen when you first set up the extension, it sends us one record per LinkedIn account, once:

  • a random ID created for your installation,
  • your name as shown on LinkedIn,
  • your LinkedIn profile ID (the part of your profile address after linkedin.com/in/),
  • the date and time you first used the extension.

This is sent to our server at referral-scout.vercel.app and stored with Vercel. We use it only to count how many people use Referral Scout. It is visible only to the developer, and it is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Services the extension uses on your behalf

  • LinkedIn. The extension works in your own logged-in LinkedIn session, doing visible things you could do yourself: searching jobs and people, reading pages, and sending invitations or messages, which need your approval unless you turn on automatic sending. LinkedIn's own privacy policy and terms apply.
  • ChatGPT. To plan searches, rank jobs and draft messages, the extension writes prompts into ChatGPT in your own ChatGPT account. These prompts can include your LinkedIn profile text, CV text, job descriptions and the public details of people you contact. OpenAI's privacy policy applies, and the conversations follow your own ChatGPT history settings. Clearing the extension does not delete them from ChatGPT.
  • OpenAI API (optional). Only if you add your own API key in Settings are the same prompts sent to the OpenAI API under your key instead.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade any data.
  • We do not show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not read your browsing history, cookies or passwords.
  • We do not run on websites other than LinkedIn and ChatGPT.

Your choices

  • By default, every invitation and message waits for your approval before it is sent.
  • You can pause the extension at any time.
  • You can delete your CV text, remove individual people, or clear all extension data from Settings.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes the data it stored in your browser.
  • To have your usage record deleted from our server, open a request at the contact link below and we will remove it.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you clear it or uninstall the extension. The usage record on our server is kept until you ask us to delete it, or until the project ends.

Children

Referral Scout is meant for job seekers aged 18 and over.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
