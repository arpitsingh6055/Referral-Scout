# Setup

1. Build with `pnpm check` and `pnpm package`.
2. Load `dist` as an unpacked extension, or reload your existing installation.
3. Refresh your LinkedIn tabs after updating.
4. Open your LinkedIn home or profile page while signed in.
5. Open Cold Contact and choose **Read my LinkedIn profile**.
6. Review the suggested target and introduction. Keep your LinkedIn profile updated.
7. Choose **Start outreach** to enable connection requests and one introduction
   after each tracked invitation is accepted.

The dashboard keeps saved profiles and their status. Use **Pause outreach** to stop
future work, **Skip** to exclude a person, and **Open profile** for conversations or
items that need attention. Pending invitations are checked every 24 hours while
Chrome is open and the computer is awake. Missed checks resume when you return.

The extension does not need a CV, Gmail setup, ChatGPT, Gemini or API credentials.
This is a preview: English LinkedIn selectors require verification in your session.
