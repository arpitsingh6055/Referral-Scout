KEY TAKEAWAYS
1. Uses LinkedIn for two main goals: expanding his network circle and finding jobs/internships (applies to ~5–6 roles per day).
2. Seeks referrals by checking the company's "About" section and mutual connections, messaging people he already knows or is connected with.
3. Maintains no external tracking system; relies solely on LinkedIn messages and notifications, which gets messy and caused him to miss a scheduled internship interview after stepping away from LinkedIn for a day.
