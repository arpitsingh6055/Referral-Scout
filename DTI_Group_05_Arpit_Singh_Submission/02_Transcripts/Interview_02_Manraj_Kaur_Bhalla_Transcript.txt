KEY TAKEAWAYS
1. Relatively new to LinkedIn; uses it informally—primarily checking profiles for skill benchmarks and occasionally messaging HR or talent acquisition leads, often receiving auto-generated replies or links to external Google Forms/portals.
2. Has not used LinkedIn referrals previously because her prior profession (architecture) relied on portfolios, physical recommendation letters, and professor referrals rather than an active LinkedIn community.
3. Identifies a clear context-recovery pain point when tailoring CVs across multiple applications: after delays of over a week, it becomes hard to recall each company's specific requirements and which tailored CV version was sent to whom.
