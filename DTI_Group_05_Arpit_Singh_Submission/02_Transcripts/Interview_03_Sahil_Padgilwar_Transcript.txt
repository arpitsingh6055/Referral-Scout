KEY TAKEAWAYS
1. Combines weekday LinkedIn feed browsing/cold emailing with weekend batch applications (10–15 targeted roles per weekend so follow-ups stay manageable).
2. Built a custom personal tracker (paired with weekend code to fetch matching jobs) logging the job posting, company, person contacted, outreach message/channel (email vs. LinkedIn), and status, cycling entries out after 1–2 weeks of follow-up.
3. Notes that before building the tracker, he lost context on whether he had applied or was waiting on a referral; observes senior employees take 4–5 days to reply (vs. 1–2 days for freshers), with low conversion (2–3 referral calls over 1–2 months).
