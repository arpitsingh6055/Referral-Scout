KEY TAKEAWAYS
1. Uses multiple channels (LinkedIn job filters, direct outreach at target startups/local firms, Monster, Naukri.com, and cold emails), peaking at 10–15 applications per day while also preparing for interviews.
2. Tracks applications manually in Excel by entering each data point himself; faces LinkedIn connection restrictions, message limits, and the time cost of drafting each outreach message manually.
3. Reports low outreach response rates (15–20 day waits, ~1–2 replies per 100 messages, ~1 positive reply per 200) and struggles to recover context on which role/JD prompted an outreach when someone replies days later.
